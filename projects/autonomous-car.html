Autonomous Car

In this project, I created a miniature autonomous car to compete in a university organized competition. I did this by using an Arduino Uno, HW870 IR Sensors, DC Motors, L298N Motor Driver, and AA Batteries.

I programmed the control logic on the Arduino using C++ to optimize wheel speed and course correction. My efforts resulted in the car being able to navigate a 9-foot linear course in 4.9 seconds, a top 5 score in a competition of ~140 students.
